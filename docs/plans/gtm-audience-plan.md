## Ideal customer profile (ICP)

**Filters to use everywhere (lists, ads, outreach):**

- Business size ~5–50 people (or multi-location small ops)
- Pain: manual email / reviews / tickets / intake eating hours weekly
- Budget: can spend **$5k–$15k** without long procurement
- Reachable decision-maker: Owner, GM, Managing Partner, Ops Manager

| Priority | Buyer | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| Best first | Restaurant / bar owners + GMs | Clear pain, volume of businesses, short decision cycle |
| Strong | Real estate team leads / brokerages | Lead follow-up is measurable |
| Strong | Small legal / accounting firm partners | High time value; intake & docs repeatable |
| Broad | Support managers at SMEs | Shared inbox / FAQ kit |

**Avoid early:** pure AI curiosity audiences, developers, big-enterprise procurement.

---

## How to find people (ranked)

### A. Warm / semi-warm (highest ROI)

1. Network + **@RyanFrequency** content: outcomes, not product features; soft CTA to site + DM.
2. Local / niche communities: chambers, hospitality groups, RE associations, bar associations, industry Facebook/LinkedIn groups. Offer free 20‑min “where AI fits” audit → sell Pilot.
3. **Partners:** web designers, POS implementers, bookkeepers, RE CRMs, MSPs (referral / finder’s fee / white-label).

### B. Named outbound lists

Build **50–100 decision-makers per vertical + metro**, not 10k random AI followers.

Sources:

- Google Maps / Yelp / industry directories
- Apollo, Clay, LinkedIn Sales Nav (Owner, GM, Managing Partner, Ops + industry)
- State bar / realtor association directories for legal & RE

Use for: cold DM/email **or** custom audiences / lookalikes later.

### C. Paid ads (after one vertical + clear offer)

| Channel | Fits | Notes |
|---------|------|--------|
| LinkedIn | Legal, accounting, RE | Expensive, clean titles |
| Meta | Local restaurant/bar owners | Geo + industry + lookalikes from site visitors |
| Google Search | Intent searchers | Job-based keywords, not “AI tools” only |
| X ads | Matches current CTA | Promote threads + site; retarget engagers |

**Targeting principle:** jobs/pain (review response, lead follow-up, client intake) or **geo + industry** — not broad AI interest.
